How the scoring works

  • Behavior score: the average of five ratings (sincerity, restraint, gratitude, benefit to others, presence) shown out of 100.
  • Mirror alignment: how consistently you kept the three practices modeled by the scholar you chose, from Not yet (0) to Consistently (2).
  • Creed-to-Commerce™ layers: Creed (belief and intention), Character (akhlaq), Conduct (daily practice) and Commerce (earning and spending). Each reflection sits in one layer so you can see where you hold firm and where creed fails to reach your commerce.
  • This is a reflection aid. It is not a fatwa, and it does not replace a qualified teacher.
